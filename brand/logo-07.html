Grapple · logo round 7

Your #3 concept, rebuilt after studying how professional marks are made (references: Taktix, HOOK by Fieon Art, Mihai Dolganiuc's eagle on Dribbble). What changed, and why the earlier rounds looked childish:

Mass, not linesOne solid shape cut by a few edges, instead of an even-width tube drawing.
Tapered, not uniformThe point is a blade that narrows to a real tip; the barb is a cut tooth. Thickness changes on purpose.
Few elementsHook, eye, and (optionally) a fine cord. No stripes, no nuts, no ridges, no drop shadows.
Gaps for overlapsThe cord threads through the eye with a hairline gap, the way the references handle overlaps.
One round elementThe eye is the only curve, so the jagged cuts read as intentional, not wobbly.
Grown-up paletteDeep forest + mint, or lime + forest, instead of bright mint + pure white. Your mint+white is still shown for comparison.
