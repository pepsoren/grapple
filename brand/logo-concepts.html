Grapple · logo concepts

Same line language as tether's knot (one rope, round caps, violet two-tone: rope #8b7cf6, hook #b4aafa), so the two apps read as a family. Each shows the big mark, the app icon, small sizes (32 / 20 / 16 px) and the wordmark.

A · Thrown hook

A grappling hook mid-throw toward the top right, rope trailing behind it. Says "launch" and "grab" at once.

B · Rope G

A G drawn in one rope; the crossbar finishes in a hook's barb. Most letter-like, cleanest at 16 px.

C · Grapnel, upright

The hook on its own, standing tall with a short tail of rope. Calm and symmetrical; the most "icon-like" of the four.

Grapple

D · tether's rope, thrown family

The rope enters from the left at the same height as tether's knot rope, loops once, and flies off into the hook. Put side by side, tether ties, Grapple throws.
